About This Exceptional Property

Welcome to 2200 Le Loup Dr, a meticulously designed luxury residence offering a perfect blend of comfort, sophistication, and space. This stunning home features an open floor plan, premium finishes, and state-of-the-art amenities throughout.

Located in Prescott's highly desirable North Forty subdivision, this exceptional 2,300-square-foot custom home was built in 2022 on nearly an acre. The moment you enter, the home's elegant design and attention to detail become apparent, with high-end materials and finishes selected throughout.

Living Spaces: The spacious great room serves as the heart of the residence, highlighted by a dramatic floor-to-ceiling stacked stone fireplace. Wood-grain Italian porcelain tile flooring flows seamlessly through the main living areas, creating an open and inviting atmosphere.

Culinary Excellence: The gourmet kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Features include an oversized island with a large farmhouse sink, custom cabinetry, stainless steel appliances, and granite countertops. A separate walkthrough pantry provides ample storage.

Primary Sanctuary: The spacious primary suite is a private retreat with direct access to the patio. Enjoy separate his-and-hers closets, dual vanities, a beautiful soaking tub, and a large walk-in shower with premium fixtures and tile work.

Construction Excellence: Exceptional energy-conscious features include foam insulation throughout exterior walls and roof (including garage), a fire sprinkler system, low-E dual-pane windows, and a 5-ton HVAC system ensuring year-round comfort and efficiency.

Outdoor Recreation: The property offers an impressive 1,200-square-foot garage accommodating two vehicles plus a Class C RV, with direct-drive access from the patio. The expansive backyard and covered patio are perfect for entertaining and enjoying Arizona's beautiful outdoor lifestyle.

Location: Just minutes from Prescott's historic Courthouse Square with its restaurants, boutiques, galleries, and entertainment. Surrounded by expansive Arizona landscapes and peaceful desert vistas, yet close to all amenities and services.
