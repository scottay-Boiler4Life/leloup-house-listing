About the Property

Welcome to 2200 Le Loup Dr. This beautiful home offers a perfect blend of comfort, style, and space. Featuring an open floor plan, a completely updated kitchen, and plenty of natural light throughout.

The outdoor area features a spacious backyard perfect for weekend gatherings. Located in a quiet, highly desirable neighborhood close to excellent schools, parks, and local amenities.

Discover refined single-level living in this exceptional 2,300-square-foot custom home, built in 2022 and set on nearly an acre in Prescott's highly desirable North Forty subdivision.

From the moment you enter, the home's attention to detail is evident. The spacious great room serves as the heart of the residence, highlighted by a dramatic floor-to-ceiling stacked stone fireplace that anchors the space.

Throughout the home, wood-grain Italian porcelain tile flooring creates a warm, elegant aesthetic while providing exceptional durability and easy maintenance.

The gourmet kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining, featuring an oversized island with a large farmhouse sink, custom cabinetry by Showcase Cabinetry, generous counter space, and premium stainless steel appliances.

The spacious primary suite is a private retreat, with direct access to the patio, separate his-and-hers closets, dual vanities, a beautifully tiled shower, and a luxurious soaking tub.

Exceptional construction and energy-conscious features include foam insulation throughout the exterior walls and roof, including the garage, a fire sprinkler system, and a re-circulating hot water system for instant hot water throughout the home.

For the car enthusiast, outdoor recreation lover, or RV owner, the property offers an impressive 1,200-square-foot garage, accommodating two vehicles plus a Class C RV. Direct-drive access from the patio adds convenience.

Life at North Forty places you just minutes from the restaurants, boutiques, galleries, and entertainment surrounding Prescott's historic Courthouse Square, while the expansive beautiful Arizona landscape creates a peaceful, private sanctuary.

A beautifully crafted, energy-conscious Prescott home offering quality, functionality, and an enviable North Forty location—designed for those who appreciate exceptional single-level living.
