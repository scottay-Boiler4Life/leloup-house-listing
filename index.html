About This Exceptional Property

Welcome to 2200 Le Loup Dr, a meticulously designed luxury residence offering a perfect blend of comfort, sophistication, and space. This stunning home features an open floor plan with premium finishes throughout, soaring ceilings, and sophisticated architectural details that create an ambiance of refined elegance.

Located in Prescott's highly desirable North Forty subdivision, this exceptional 2,300-square-foot custom home was built in 2022 on nearly an acre of pristine Arizona landscape. The moment you enter, the home's superior construction quality and attention to detail become immediately apparent, setting this residence apart as a true luxury retreat.

Living Spaces: The spacious great room serves as the heart of the residence, highlighted by a dramatic floor-to-ceiling stacked stone fireplace. Wood-grain Italian porcelain tile flooring flows throughout the main living areas, creating seamless transitions between spaces. Large windows frame the spectacular Arizona views and flood the interior with natural light.

Culinary Excellence: The gourmet kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Features include an oversized island with a large farmhouse sink, custom cabinetry, premium stainless steel appliances, stone countertops, and a convenient walk-in pantry. The kitchen opens directly to the living area, perfect for modern family living and entertaining guests.

Primary Sanctuary: The spacious primary suite is a private retreat with direct access to the patio. Enjoy separate his-and-hers closets, dual vanities, a beautiful walk-in shower with premium fixtures, and a luxurious soaking tub. The suite design prioritizes comfort and elegance at every turn.

Construction Excellence: Exceptional energy-conscious features include foam insulation throughout exterior walls and roof (including garage), a fire sprinkler system for maximum safety, and a tankless water heater for efficiency. The home combines luxury with smart building practices that minimize environmental impact while maximizing comfort.

Outdoor Recreation: The property offers an impressive 1,200-square-foot garage accommodating two vehicles plus a Class C RV, with direct-drive access from the patio area. The expansive outdoor spaces are perfect for entertaining, with multiple patios offering stunning views of the surrounding landscape. Native landscaping enhances the natural Arizona aesthetic.

Location: Just minutes from Prescott's historic Courthouse Square with its restaurants, boutiques, galleries, and entertainment venues. The North Forty community offers the perfect balance of peaceful seclusion and convenient access to all Prescott has to offer. Surrounded by expansive Arizona landscape and mountain views, you'll experience the best of desert living.
