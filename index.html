About This Exceptional Property

Welcome to 2200 Le Loup Dr, a meticulously designed luxury residence offering a perfect blend of comfort, sophistication, and space. This stunning home features an open floor plan, high-end finishes, and modern amenities designed for the discerning buyer seeking an Arizona lifestyle retreat.

Located in Prescott's highly desirable North Forty subdivision, this exceptional 2,300-square-foot custom home was built in 2022 on nearly an acre. The moment you enter, the home's elegant aesthetic and meticulous attention to detail become apparent, with premium materials and thoughtful design throughout.

Living Spaces: The spacious great room serves as the heart of the residence, highlighted by a dramatic floor-to-ceiling stacked stone fireplace. Wood-grain Italian porcelain tile flows seamlessly throughout, creating a cohesive and sophisticated aesthetic. Floor-to-ceiling windows frame Arizona's natural beauty.

Culinary Excellence: The gourmet kitchen is designed for both everyday living and entertaining. Features include an oversized island with a large farmhouse sink, custom cabinetry, premium stainless steel appliances, and abundant natural light. The kitchen opens to the main living areas, perfect for hosting.

Primary Sanctuary: The spacious primary suite is a private retreat with direct access to the patio. Enjoy separate his-and-hers closets, dual vanities, a beautiful walk-in shower with natural stone, and heated floors for ultimate comfort.

Construction Excellence: Exceptional energy-conscious features include foam insulation throughout exterior walls and roof (including garage), a fire sprinkler system, smart home pre-wiring, and premium HVAC systems designed for Arizona's climate.

Outdoor Recreation: The property offers an impressive 1,200-square-foot garage accommodating two vehicles plus a Class C RV, with direct-drive access from the patio. Multiple outdoor living spaces showcase Arizona's dramatic sunsets and mountain vistas.

Location: Just minutes from Prescott's historic Courthouse Square with its restaurants, boutiques, galleries, and entertainment. Surrounded by expansive Arizona landscape with privacy and natural beauty at your doorstep.
